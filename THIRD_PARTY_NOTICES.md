## GeoNames India Gazetteer

`assets/india-places.db` contains transformed GeoNames India place and postal data dated 22 November 2026. The original archives are the [India gazetteer](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/IN.zip) and [India postal extract](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/IN.zip). © [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). DriveSentry selected populated places and administrative divisions, indexed published names and aliases, formatted administrative context and stored the resulting coordinates and PIN entries in SQLite. Coordinates represent search locations, not individual buildings.
